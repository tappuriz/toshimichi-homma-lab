Research

From expertise to organizational strength.

How can professionals and non-professionals draw on each other’s knowledge and roles to collaborate better? I study occupational and organizational commitment and voluntary contribution to the organization.

Current

Current themes

I study how people relate to their occupation and organization, extra-role behavior, and organizational culture— in settings involving hospital staff, community pharmacists, and pharmacy managers.

Through surveys and interviews, I aim to clarify the conditions under which professionals’ knowledge and sense of responsibility translate into collaboration and contribution to the organization.

Focus

Research lenses

01

Occupational & organizational commitment

How attachment to work and to the employing organization, intentions to remain, and felt responsibility are expressed in everyday behavior.

02

Professional and non-professional collaboration

Conditions that help people with different expertise—such as pharmacists and clerical staff in community pharmacies—work together using each other’s knowledge.

03

Extra-role behavior

Behavior beyond formal role requirements—support for colleagues, workplace improvement—and its relationship to collaboration and organizational performance.
