What I do

Three pillars of the lab

01

Research

Grounded in organizational behavior, with a focus on occupational commitment, extra-role behavior, and the management of professional work.

02

Seminar

I teach organizational behavior and theory and supervise both undergraduate and graduate students, with an emphasis on research exercises connected to external study groups and data science events.

03

Commons

I host Management & AI Commons Osaka—lightning talks and work sessions on AI, management, and organizations in Osaka.
